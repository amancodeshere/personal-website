## Food illustration prompt

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the image, saved in this project as `public/projects/recipe-food.webp` after WebP compression. The final prompt was:

> Create a beautiful, realistic food photograph for a software portfolio's recipe recommendation project. Landscape wide composition, overhead flat lay of three separate ceramic bowls arranged left to right on a warm pale stone tabletop: left a rich tomato and red lentil stew garnished with herbs, center a spinach and chickpea curry with fresh ginger and cilantro, right sesame vegetable noodles with bok choy and carrots. Each dish occupies one third of the composition, generous clear distinction between dishes, bowls fully visible. Professional natural window light, tactile real food, appetizing rich earthy colors, editorial cookbook photography. Absolutely no typography, labels, interface, decorations, logos, hands or people. This will be used as illustrative food imagery, not an actual application screenshot. Wide 3:1 photographic triptych, identical camera angle and scale for all three dishes.

## Additional repository projects

- **Music-Rec:** authored topic-profile and recommendation study based on the public notebook/README. Track and artist names and topic weights are fictional examples, not dataset excerpts or evaluation metrics.
- **Data Exfiltration Prevention:** authored packet activity and alert study based on AwesomeDEP's Scapy/SQLite workflow. All displayed events are synthetic; no real addresses, packet payloads, or logs are included. The preview uses the portfolio's deep ocean-blue surfaces, icy text, and cyan indicators; amber is reserved for warning labels and the alert node border. These colours are shared across the homepage, archive, and detail view.
- **sudo_win:** `public/projects/sudo-win-battlecode-board.webp` is the actual Schooltime board at round 80 from saved match 830316. Official match metadata identifies it as sudo win vs winner on 1 October. The locally retained replay was opened in the installed official `unswbc.battledragon-replay-viewer` 1.1.0 renderer; its native “Save the board as an image” export was converted to WebP. The replay itself and renderer code are not shipped. [Official match metadata](https://game.battlecode.au/visualiser?match=830316), [official renderer source](https://github.com/unswcpmsoc/unswbc/tree/main/vscode-extension).
- **Botfluencer:** `public/projects/ctn-official-competition.webp` is a real capture of the [official Capture the Narrative homepage](https://capturethenarrative.com/), including its competition artwork and UNSW logos. It represents the competition rather than an implemented Botfluencer dashboard. The [2026 official guide](https://capturethenarrative.com/info/competitions/2026/getting-started/) identifies Legit Social as the competition platform. No claim of live Botfluencer publishing or a deployed worker is implied.
- **Spin the Block:** `public/projects/spin-the-block.webp`, converted from the public repository's `game-audit/evidence/beta-test-phase4/02_desktop_1920x1080_viewport.png`. The capture shows the actual game board and test crews. Source: https://github.com/amancodeshere/spin-the-block/blob/HEAD/game-audit/evidence/beta-test-phase4/02_desktop_1920x1080_viewport.png
- **Midnight Run:** `public/projects/midnight-run.webp`, captured by rendering the public pinball frontend locally. The original playfield is visible in a started, unlaunched game; the score service was absent. Only public frontend files were served, without the backend or database. Source: https://github.com/amancodeshere/pinball

All additions use the shared caption-free artwork wrapper. Remaining authored study styles are isolated in `AdditionalProjectArtwork.module.css`; breakpoint decisions follow the artwork container width.

Recipe dish framing isolates one square panel of the food triptych before applying a centered 118% crop. Each dish has its own focal position; narrow 100px thumbnails retain a 118px crop floor so neighbouring dishes and empty edges cannot enter the frame.

JobForge displays the complete existing 1280×720 capture at its native 16:9 ratio, with `fit: "contain"`. Optional image `aspectRatio` and `fit` metadata support full-image framing per project without changing other artwork.
